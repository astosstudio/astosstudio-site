Binairo tip

Ingepland dinsdag 24 november, 18:30 · Facebook + Instagram · 1 afbeelding
Tekst 
Daily Puzzle Club tip 🧩 Binairo edition.

Two of the same next to each other? The squares on both ends must be the opposite, because three in a row isn't allowed.

The same goes for a gap: 0 _ 0 means the middle has to be a 1.

Save this one for the next puzzle break.
