Support

Questions, a problem or an idea? Email us and we usually reply within two working days.

support@astosstudio.com

Please mention which game it’s about, your phone model and iOS or Android version, your player ID (in the game’s Settings), and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

Keep your progress

Link your account to an e-mail address: Settings › Account › Link. We send you a 6-digit code. There is no password.

New phone? Open Settings › Account, choose I already have an account and sign in with the same address.

Restore purchases

New phone or reinstalled the game? Open Settings › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancel VIP

iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them.

iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: your Google Play order history.

Delete your account

In the game: Settings › Delete account and data. This removes everything from our server.

Can’t open the game anymore? Email us your player ID or player name and we’ll delete it for you. How deleting works.

Report or block a player

Tap a player, a message or a family and choose Report or Block. Our staff look at every report.

Messages from blocked players are hidden. Undo a block in Settings.

Private messages

Choose who can message you in Settings: everyone, your family or nobody.

Private messages unlock after mission 10, and messages are deleted after 30 days.

Notifications

Turn notifications on or off in Settings › Notifications. You get at most two a day, and never at night.

No notifications at all? Check that they are allowed for Mob Holdout in your phone’s settings.

Connection problems

Mob Holdout is an online game. Without a connection the game pauses. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap Try again.

If the connection stays away during a battle, the game asks you to restart and that battle ends. A City raid in progress is settled up to where you were.

Language

The game is in 14 languages and picks one based on where you are. Choose another in Settings › Language.

Your choice is saved with your account.
