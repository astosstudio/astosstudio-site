Patch notes

Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

A mafia lane-defense game. Hold your street with one turret against wave after wave of rival crews, build your city and raid other players’ estates.

About the gameAll patch notesSupport

15 updates with 265 changes. The game is in development; it isn’t in the stores yet.

  1. 0.1517 November 2026

    Sound, skins and raids on your estateLatest

    Sound and music

    • NewMusic: one track for your city, one for battle and one for boss fights.
    • NewRecorded sounds for your turret: shots, the shotgun, the sniper, rockets, the flamethrower and critical hits.
    • ImprovedMusic follows your phone’s silent switch and plays alongside other apps. Switch it off in Settings.

    City raid

    • NewA raid now plays on your target’s own estate: the mansion, the vault with the loot, the garage and the armory stand behind the wall.
    • NewYou see the turret skin of the player you raid, and raiders see yours.
    • NewTower range shows while you pick a lane. After the first mine goes off, you see the mines that are left in that lane.
    • NewBig moments in a raid: a tower falls, the gate breaks, a star lands and loot leaves the vault.
    • ImprovedThe clock turns red and ticks in the last ten seconds.

    Skins

    • NewFive Untouchable skins above Legendary: Black Opal, Liquid Chrome, Royal Flush, Hologram and Capo di tutti capi.
    • NewEach Untouchable skin gives a small bonus on your own street, such as damage, fire rate, wall or coins.
    • NewBonuses from all the skins you own add up, whichever skin you wear.
    • NewUntouchable skins have moving materials and their own effect when an enemy falls. Each comes with a profile banner and a title.
    • NewOther players see your skin on your profile, and rare skins show next to your name on the leaderboard.
    • ImprovedFrom Mythic up, the ring around your turret and the muzzle flash take the color of your skin.

    Your estate

    • ImprovedThe mansion comes first. It starts at level 1 and now goes up to level 25.
    • ImprovedNo building can be a higher level than your mansion.
    • ImprovedThe mansion can level up once four other buildings have reached its level.

    In a run

    • NewTake all 3: watch an ad on an upgrade choice to get all three cards, up to three times per run.
    • ImprovedGame speed is 1× for everyone. Family VIP plays at 2×.
    • ImprovedThe 4× speed in Endless is gone.
    • ImprovedThe 3D battle look is now on for everyone. You can switch back to the flat look in Settings.

    Account and connection

    • NewLink your account to an e-mail address in Settings, so you keep your progress on a new phone.
    • NewSign in with your e-mail on another phone to pick up where you left off.
    • ImprovedIf your connection drops during a fight, the fight pauses. If it stays away, the game asks you to restart.
    • ImprovedA City raid that was running is saved up to where you were and settled after the restart.

    Help and text

    • ImprovedHow to play has ten cards, including building, raiding, Capos, currencies and where to find everything.
    • ImprovedThe guided tour now points to Capos, your profile with the album, and the districts.
    • ImprovedShorter lines under names in lists. The longer explanation sits behind the question mark.
    • ImprovedCapos and Sal the shopkeeper now breathe and blink.
    • ImprovedYour Capo slides into view, full size, when you fire a super attack.
    • ImprovedNotifications on iPhone show the name Mob Holdout as their title.

    Fixes

    • FixedOn small phones, the Claim all button in the season pass sat behind the bottom bar.
    • FixedOn iPhones with a Dynamic Island, the name of a raid target covered the top of the estate.
    • FixedFamily VIP showed “Demo: nothing is charged” in the app.
    • FixedA Japanese label broke in the middle of a word.
  2. 0.1414 November 2026

    Neon with weight

    Look and feel

    • NewEverything you can tap is now an object: a dark outline, light from above and a lip that presses in.
    • NewAll 198 icons are redrawn as filled, colored objects.
    • NewRewards build up in three beats: suspense, the reveal, then what you get, line by line.
    • NewA new estate rank, reputation title or raid division gets its own moment.
    • NewCoins and diamonds fly to their counter when you earn them.
    • NewUpgrade cards have a color by kind: attack, defense, support or evolution.
    • ImprovedThe result screen shows your stars landing one by one. The numbers sit behind Details.
    • ImprovedScreens open from the button you tapped, and their rows slide in one after another.

    Battle

    • NewAll 17 enemy types are small 3D figures, seen from above at an angle: runners, bikes, cars, trucks, drones and eight bosses.
    • NewYour turret, the wall and everything in your yard are drawn in the same style, in every skin.
    • NewYour crew in a raid, your dog, your armored car and your helicopter are 3D figures too.
    • NewTwo new streets: the Night market and the Bridge. Missions now rotate through five streets.
    • ImprovedLess on screen during a run: the wave is large in the middle, with one mark per wave.
    • ImprovedThe mission name and your kill count moved to the pause screen.
    • ImprovedRockets, grenades and explosions have weight. Bullets, sparks and weather stay light.

    Getting around

    • ImprovedFive spots at the bottom: Shop, Capos, City, Family and Events. The More tab is gone.
    • ImprovedStatistics and the Album are in your profile. How to play and Follow us are in Settings.
    • NewThe mission map is a road through the city. It starts at the bottom and winds up to the chapter boss.
    • NewThe shop is a place: Sal the bookkeeper stands behind the counter and reacts to what you do.
    • ImprovedShop items are tiles, six in view. Tap one for the details.
    • ImprovedExplanations moved behind a question mark at the top of each screen.

    Characters

    • NewThe eight Capos and Don Corvino are redrawn as half-length figures with three expressions.
    • NewThe Capos screen is a collection. Capos you don’t own yet show as a silhouette.
    • NewA new app icon: the Don.

    Sound

    • New32 recorded sound effects for buttons, rewards, stars, explosions and more.
    • ImprovedEvery button outside battle has its own tap sound.

    Players and fair play

    • NewView another player’s profile from the leaderboard, chat or your family.
    • ImprovedYour diamonds are now kept on the server.
    • ImprovedFamily boss damage is counted by the server.
    • ImprovedA family’s join code is now private. Only the Boss and rank 4 can see it.
    • ImprovedThe game checks in with the server during play, and your saved progress is verified.
    • ImprovedOne device at a time. If another phone is playing on your account, you choose where to continue.
    • ImprovedChanging your name waits for the server before it charges diamonds.

    Skins

    • ImprovedCamouflage and Night Vision still cost coins. The other 22 skins now cost diamonds.
    • ImprovedSkins you already owned stay yours. Earned skins stay earned.

    Fixes

    • FixedA player with Lucia could beat the Family boss with a single super attack.
    • FixedAn empty strip sat above the bottom bar on most screens.
    • FixedThe ×2 label on the Defend button was cut off.
    • FixedThe counter on your portrait showed as a red stripe across your name.
    • FixedThe amount in the welcome chest ignored your language’s number format.
  3. 0.1312 November 2026

    Your first week

    First week

    • NewA seven-day path with three goals a day. A new day opens each calendar day.
    • NewFour rewards along the way. Finish 18 goals to unlock Capo Vito.
    • NewNotifications on your phone: your shield is ending, the Night watch is full, your daily bonus is ready.
    • ImprovedAt most two notifications a day, and never between 22:00 and 8:00.
    • ImprovedThe game asks before your phone does. Change it any time in Settings.

    Friends

    • NewInvite friends with your own six-character code.
    • NewYou earn coins and diamonds as a friend reaches missions 5, 10, 20, 30 and 50.
    • NewChests at one, three and six friends. Your friend gets a welcome gift.
    • NewFollow us: links to our website, Instagram and Facebook, with a small thank-you for your first visit.

    Shop

    • NewFour packs that appear when you reach something: your first raid, a Capo with two stars, a three-star raid and a higher estate rank.
    • NewThe Growth fund: buy it once and collect diamonds at missions 10 through 100.
    • NewA sixth diamond pack.
    • ImprovedThe Starter Pack now appears after mission 6.
    • ImprovedAn offer is no longer lost if you just saw another one. It waits in the shop.
    • ImprovedOffer windows only open on the home screen, never over another screen.
    • ImprovedNo more made-up “was” prices on offers.
    • ImprovedDouble diamonds on your first purchase now applies to the smallest pack only.

    Rewards

    • ImprovedFree diamonds from the daily bonus, contracts, achievements, the season pass and the wheel are about half of what they were.
  4. 0.1211 November 2026

    The city is home

    Home screen

    • NewYou now open the game in your 3D city.
    • NewThree big buttons: Attack, Defend and Build.
    • NewOne strip at the top shows your next step.
    • NewBuild opens a list of all ten buildings, with what you can afford first.
    • ImprovedEvents, the season pass and mail sit in a drawer on the right.

    One style

    • NewThe whole game has one neon style: night blue, purple and neon.
    • NewColor has a fixed meaning: cyan for the main action, pink for attacking, green for what you can do now, gold for money, rank and premium.
    • NewOur own icons and Capo portraits replace emoji everywhere.
    • NewEvery screen has the same frame, with Back at the top left.
    • NewThe coin has an A on it.
    • ImprovedThree or more notifications arrive together as one bundle.
    • ImprovedLocked features are hidden. One line tells you what opens next.
    • ImprovedAll text is at least 12 points, and every tab and small button is 44 points high.

    First minutes

    • NewA guided start: after the prologue you build the Gate, then play mission 2.
    • NewYour first City raid is guided step by step.
    • ImprovedThe prologue can’t be skipped any more.
    • ImprovedMissions 1 to 3 have 6 waves, 4 and 5 have 8, and 6 to 9 have 10.
    • ImprovedEarly missions have more enemies on screen, each a little weaker.
    • ImprovedYou can’t lose missions 1 to 3.
    • ImprovedCity raid now opens after mission 3. Other players can raid you from mission 10.
    • ImprovedYour first real win gets its own line on the result screen.

    Result screen

    • ImprovedThe result comes first. Reward windows wait until you tap the main button.

    Languages

    • NewThe game is in 14 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
    • NewThe language is picked from where you are. Change it in Settings.

    Fixes

    • FixedOn iPhone, the top bar sat too low and the bottom bar too high.
    • FixedSix names stayed in English in every language.
    • FixedA tip in the prologue could be skipped for good if an upgrade choice opened at the same moment.
    • FixedThe Defend button could catch taps meant for a building’s panel.
  5. 0.119 November 2026

    City raid

    City raid

    • NewCity raid: attack other players’ estates. Send your crew down three lanes to break the gate and the towers.
    • NewEarn up to three stars, loot and trophies. A defender never loses coins.
    • NewAfter a raid with two or three stars, the defender gets a shield for 8 hours.
    • NewPick a squad of four out of nine kinds of men, and train them.
    • NewYour active Capo joins the raid with a super attack.
    • NewDivisions from Bronze to Godfather, with a season reward.
    • NewA daily chest after three raids won.
    • NewWatch a replay of every raid, on you or by you.
    • NewTake revenge, or hold your own street against the crew that raided you.
    • NewDefend your estate with traps in a lane you choose, and give it its own skin.
    • NewScout a target’s estate in 3D before you attack.
    • ImprovedFamily wars are now fought on the estates of the other family’s members.

    Your city

    • NewYour estate is a 3D city at night. Each of the ten buildings changes as it levels up.
    • NewTap a building to upgrade it. Drag, pinch and zoom to look around.
    • NewThe Family house and the Crew tower stand in your city.
    • NewThe daily bonus is a seven-day calendar.
    • NewEvents has a calendar: what is on now, what is always open and what is coming.

    Mail and chat

    • NewMail: messages, purchases, family news, battle reports, event rewards and news from the studio in one place.
    • NewCollect rewards you missed from Mail.
    • ImprovedChat looks like a messaging app: bubbles, times, date lines and a pinned announcement.
    • ImprovedChat is plain text with emoji from the game’s own picker. Quick messages are gone.
    • ImprovedThe Family tab has two halves: Family and Your crew.

    Screens

    • ImprovedSharper graphics, picked for your phone.
    • ImprovedFoldables and tablets show the game as an arcade cabinet instead of black bars.
    • ImprovedLoading screens show the shape of what is coming.
  6. 0.107 November 2026

    Family and Diamonds

    Diamonds

    • NewDiamonds are a new rare currency.
    • NewEarn diamonds from boss missions, chapter bosses and your first 3 stars on a mission.
    • NewAchievements now give diamonds instead of coins.
    • NewReputation milestones, the season pass and family quests give diamonds too.
    • NewThe Shop has a Diamonds tab with five diamond packs.
    • NewYour first purchase of each pack gives double diamonds.
    • NewThe coin exchange turns diamonds into coins. It is always worth a fixed amount of your own income.
    • NewSpend diamonds on Capo shards, a second chance, double Night watch rewards and boosts.
    • NewPremium skins can now be bought with diamonds.
    • NewThe Starter Pack now includes 300 diamonds, items, the Neon Noir skin and 20 legendary Capo shards.
    • NewFamily VIP now adds 10 diamonds a day to the VIP chest.
    • ImprovedCoins are no longer sold directly. Buy diamonds and exchange them for coins.
    • ImprovedCapo shards and boosts now cost diamonds instead of coins.
    • ImprovedYour first name change is free. After that, a name change costs 100 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe top bar shows your diamonds next to your coins.
    • ImprovedBig numbers in the top bar are shorter, like 1.26M, so your name always fits.

    Daily and weekly rewards

    • ImprovedThe daily bonus now gives items, Capo shards, a boost and diamonds.
    • ImprovedDay 7 of the daily bonus gives 15 diamonds and 10 legendary Capo shards.
    • ImprovedContracts now give Capo shards and SP. Finish all three for 3 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe daily challenge now gives 2 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe wheel of fortune has new prizes: shards, items, SP, Honor and diamonds. The jackpot is 50 diamonds.
    • ImprovedSurprises on your estate now give items, Capo shards or coins.
    • ImprovedWeekly rewards grow with your income, so they stay worth it as you progress.
    • ImprovedBeating the Family boss or winning the faction war or a family war also gives 10 diamonds.

    Family

    • NewThe family screen is now an overview with tiles: Chat, Help, Gifts, Quests, Store, Research, Donors, Faction war, Ranking, Members, Requests and Log.
    • NewLeaders can post an announcement for the whole family.
    • NewAsk your family for help with items or coins. Help others to earn coins and Honor.
    • NewHonor is a new family currency. Earn it by helping, donating, gifts and quests.
    • NewThe family store sells items, coins, Capo shards, a family chest and a profile banner for Honor.
    • NewFamily gifts: when a member buys something or beats the Family boss, everyone gets a chest.
    • NewWeekly family quests with three reward steps.
    • NewFamily ranking by power, faction points this week and wars won.
    • ImprovedFamilies now unlock after mission 5. You get a message when they open.
    • ImprovedThe team field on the name screen is gone. Your tag is now your family tag.

    Chat

    • NewWrite your own messages in family chat. Quick messages and emotes still work.
    • NewSend private messages to other players. Tap a player and choose Message.
    • NewFind your conversations under More › Messages.
    • NewA Translate button appears under messages in another language.
    • NewChoose who can message you in Settings: everyone, your family or nobody.
    • ImprovedPrivate messages unlock after mission 10.

    Safety and fair play

    • NewReport or block players and families. Tap a player, a message or a family.
    • NewBlocked players show as “Blocked player” in rankings, and their messages are hidden. Blocked families disappear from search and rankings.
    • NewUndo a block in Settings.
    • NewOur Terms of use are in Settings and on the name screen.
    • NewYour player ID is in Settings. Share it when you contact support.
    • ImprovedOffensive names and messages are blocked.
    • ImprovedFor your safety, messages with phone numbers, e-mail addresses or links are not sent.
    • ImprovedNames like admin, moderator and dev are reserved for the Mob Holdout team.
    • ImprovedLeaderboard scores and family contributions are now checked. Impossible scores no longer count.
    • ImprovedChanging your phone’s clock no longer gives extra rewards.
    • ImprovedMob Holdout now needs an internet connection. Your run pauses when you go offline.

    Progress and balance

    • ImprovedMissions have a new difficulty curve. Every mission up to 1000 can be beaten again.
    • ImprovedPermanent upgrades now count for half in Missions.
    • ImprovedIn Endless, enemies get tougher after threat 40, so every run comes to an end.
    • ImprovedEndless coin rewards slowly drop after 20 minutes, to at most 35% less.
    • ImprovedAUTO in Endless now plays at up to 2× speed.
    • ImprovedDistrict income is lower, and districts unlock one by one as you progress in Missions.
    • ImprovedThe district vault now fills for up to 24 hours.
    • ImprovedPermanent upgrades above level 25 cost a little more each level.
    • ImprovedThe leaderboard unlocks after mission 10.

    Look and feel

    • NewA guided tour shows where new features are and what to do next.
    • NewDisplay quality setting: Auto, High, Balanced or Battery saver. Auto picks the best sharpness for your phone.
    • ImprovedAfter the prologue you choose your name and go straight to mission 2.
    • ImprovedNotifications wait until a tip or the tour is finished.
    • ImprovedGame cards no longer show a NEW label. The Heist card shows your heists won.
    • ImprovedThe mission card has a big, full-width mission button.
    • ImprovedCoins and kills in the HUD have fixed icons.
    • ImprovedDefend together and Spectate are hidden until they are ready.

    Fixes

    • FixedDeleting your account now removes all your progress for good.
    • FixedExtra barrel bullets now meet at the target instead of spreading out and missing.
    • FixedAn invisible chest button covered the Play button.
    • FixedThe game cards no longer jump to the daily challenge by themselves.
    • FixedWrong texts on the first run of the day and the event title.
    • FixedThe name screen no longer cuts off its corners.
    • FixedShared score cards now use the name Mob Holdout.
    • FixedNotifications no longer cover tutorial tips.
    • FixedSome icons showed as emoji.
    • FixedThe district income shown now matches what you get.
