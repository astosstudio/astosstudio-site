Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

Mob Holdout is a fast, neon-soaked defense game. Man the turret behind your wall, pick the right upgrades and stop wave after wave of gangsters, armored trucks and mafia bosses before they break through.

BUILD YOUR CITY

Your estate is your home base: a glowing 3D city that grows with you. Upgrade your mansion, vault, towers and more, and collect income while you are away.

Hold your street, stop the mob

The mob is coming for your street. You are the last line of defense.

BUILD YOUR ARSENAL

Every fight is different. Choose from dozens of upgrades: chain lightning, ricochet bullets, drone support, explosive rounds, mortars and more. Combine them into powerful evolutions.

RAID RIVAL ESTATES

Pick your squad, break through the gate and take the loot. Climb the divisions, earn trophies and defend your own estate against other players.

RECRUIT LEGENDARY CAPOS

Unlock eight Capos, each with a unique super attack, perks and mastery levels. Charge the ultimate and turn the fight around.

1,000 MISSIONS AND ENDLESS MODE

Fight through 1,000 missions across 20 chapters and earn three stars on each. Or see how long you can hold out in Endless.

LEAD YOUR FAMILY

Found or join a family. Chat, help each other, research bonuses together and take on the weekly Family boss.

SEASONS AND EVENTS

A 50-tier season pass, a season boss, daily rewards and events.

FAIR TO PLAY

No energy system. Earn everything by playing. Optional purchases and rewarded ads help you progress faster, and are never required.
