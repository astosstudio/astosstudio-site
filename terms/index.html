Terms of use

Last updated: 7 November 2026

These terms apply when you play Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense. By playing you accept them. If you got the game from the App Store, Apple’s standard licence agreement (EULA) also applies; on Google Play, Google’s terms apply.

Who we are

Mob Holdout is made by AstosStudio, a Dutch sole proprietorship registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 93013116. Contact: support@astosstudio.com.

No tolerance for offensive content

Other players can see your player name, your family name, tag and motto, family announcements, your messages in family chat and the private messages you send them. None of this may be offensive. We do not tolerate:

  • hate, discrimination, threats, bullying or harassment;
  • sexual, violent or otherwise shocking content;
  • pretending to be a member of the Mob Holdout team;
  • personal information about other people, advertising or spam;
  • asking other players for personal details or photos, or to move to another app.

Chat and private messages

For your safety, names and messages with offensive words are blocked, and messages with phone numbers, email addresses, links or names of social media apps are not sent. You can send private messages from mission 10, one day after you start playing. In Settings › Private messages you choose who can message you: everyone, only your family, or nobody.

Report and block

Tap a player in the leaderboard, a member of your family, a chat message, a private message or another family, then choose Report or Block. Blocked players can no longer send you private messages, and their messages and names are hidden for you. Blocked families disappear from search and rankings. You can undo a block in Settings › Blocked players and families.

We review every report within 24 hours. If content breaks these terms, we remove it and suspend the player or family that posted it. A suspended account can no longer appear in rankings, families or chat. You can also email us at support@astosstudio.com.

Fair play

Do not cheat, use bots or modified versions of the game, or abuse bugs. We check leaderboard scores and family points: a score that is not possible in the time you played does not count, and the game uses the time of our server, not your phone’s clock. We may reset scores or suspend accounts that cheat. A person always decides on a suspension.

Purchases and virtual items

Diamonds, coins and other in-game items have no value outside the game and cannot be exchanged for money. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google under their terms, and refunds go through their store. A subscription such as Family VIP renews until you cancel it in your store settings. Deleting your game account does not cancel it.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. You choose to watch them for an in-game reward.

Your account

The game creates an anonymous account for you. You can delete it at any time in Settings › Delete account and data, or read how on deleting your account. How we handle your data is explained in our privacy policy.

Changes and liability

We may update the game and these terms. We announce important changes in the game. The game is provided as it is; our liability is limited as far as Dutch law allows. Dutch law applies.
