How we make games

  • Fair by design

    No pay-to-win walls and no dark patterns. You decide what a game is worth to you.

  • Made for short sessions

    A wave on the train, a puzzle with your coffee. Every game is built for iPhone and Android from the start.

  • One maker, one inbox

    AstosStudio is one developer in the Netherlands. Your message goes straight to the person who made the game.
