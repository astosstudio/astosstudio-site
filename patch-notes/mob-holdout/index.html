Patch notes

Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

A mafia lane-defense game. Rival crews storm your wall, wave after wave, and you have one turret to hold the line.

About the gameAll patch notesSupport

10 updates with 121 changes. The game is in development; it isn’t in the stores yet.

  1. 0.107 November 2026

    Family and DiamondsLatest

    Diamonds

    • NewDiamonds are a new rare currency.
    • NewEarn diamonds from boss missions, chapter bosses and your first 3 stars on a mission.
    • NewAchievements now give diamonds instead of coins.
    • NewReputation milestones, the season pass and family quests give diamonds too.
    • NewThe Shop has a Diamonds tab with five diamond packs.
    • NewYour first purchase of each pack gives double diamonds.
    • NewThe coin exchange turns diamonds into coins. It is always worth a fixed amount of your own income.
    • NewSpend diamonds on Capo shards, a second chance, double Night watch rewards and boosts.
    • NewPremium skins can now be bought with diamonds.
    • NewThe Starter Pack now includes 300 diamonds, items, the Neon Noir skin and 20 legendary Capo shards.
    • NewFamily VIP now adds 10 diamonds a day to the VIP chest.
    • ImprovedCoins are no longer sold directly. Buy diamonds and exchange them for coins.
    • ImprovedCapo shards and boosts now cost diamonds instead of coins.
    • ImprovedYour first name change is free. After that, a name change costs 100 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe top bar shows your diamonds next to your coins.
    • ImprovedBig numbers in the top bar are shorter, like 1.26M, so your name always fits.

    Daily and weekly rewards

    • ImprovedThe daily bonus now gives items, Capo shards, a boost and diamonds.
    • ImprovedDay 7 of the daily bonus gives 15 diamonds and 10 legendary Capo shards.
    • ImprovedContracts now give Capo shards and SP. Finish all three for 3 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe daily challenge now gives 2 diamonds.
    • ImprovedThe wheel of fortune has new prizes: shards, items, SP, Honor and diamonds. The jackpot is 50 diamonds.
    • ImprovedArcade cabinets now give Capo shards, SP and Honor.
    • ImprovedSurprises on your estate now give items, Capo shards or coins.
    • ImprovedWeekly rewards grow with your income, so they stay worth it as you progress.
    • ImprovedBeating the Family boss or winning the tournament, the faction war or a family war also gives 10 diamonds.

    Family

    • NewThe family screen is now an overview with tiles: Chat, Help, Gifts, Quests, Store, Research, Donors, Faction war, Ranking, Members, Requests and Log.
    • NewLeaders can post an announcement for the whole family.
    • NewAsk your family for help with items or coins. Help others to earn coins and Honor.
    • NewHonor is a new family currency. Earn it by helping, donating, gifts and quests.
    • NewThe family store sells items, coins, Capo shards, a family chest and a profile banner for Honor.
    • NewFamily gifts: when a member buys something or beats the Family boss, everyone gets a chest.
    • NewWeekly family quests with three reward steps.
    • NewFamily ranking by power, faction points this week and wars won.
    • ImprovedFamilies now unlock after mission 5. You get a message when they open.
    • ImprovedThe team field on the name screen is gone. Your tag is now your family tag.

    Chat

    • NewWrite your own messages in family chat. Quick messages and emotes still work.
    • NewSend private messages to other players. Tap a player and choose Message.
    • NewFind your conversations under More › Messages.
    • NewA Translate button appears under messages in another language.
    • NewChoose who can message you in Settings: everyone, your family or nobody.
    • ImprovedPrivate messages unlock after mission 10.

    Safety and fair play

    • NewReport or block players and families. Tap a player, a message or a family.
    • NewBlocked players show as “Blocked player” in rankings, and their messages are hidden. Blocked families disappear from search and rankings.
    • NewUndo a block in Settings.
    • NewOur Terms of use are in Settings and on the name screen.
    • NewYour player ID is in Settings. Share it when you contact support.
    • ImprovedOffensive names and messages are blocked.
    • ImprovedFor your safety, messages with phone numbers, e-mail addresses or links are not sent.
    • ImprovedNames like admin, moderator and dev are reserved for the Mob Holdout team.
    • ImprovedLeaderboard scores and family contributions are now checked. Impossible scores no longer count.
    • ImprovedChanging your phone’s clock no longer gives extra rewards.
    • ImprovedMob Holdout now needs an internet connection. Your run pauses when you go offline.

    Progress and balance

    • ImprovedMissions have a new difficulty curve. Every mission up to 1000 can be beaten again.
    • ImprovedPermanent upgrades now count for half in Missions.
    • ImprovedIn Endless, enemies get tougher after threat 40, so every run comes to an end.
    • ImprovedEndless coin rewards slowly drop after 20 minutes, to at most 35% less.
    • ImprovedAUTO in Endless now plays at up to 2× speed.
    • ImprovedDistrict income is lower, and districts unlock one by one as you progress in Missions.
    • ImprovedThe district vault now fills for up to 24 hours.
    • ImprovedPermanent upgrades above level 25 cost a little more each level.
    • ImprovedThe leaderboard unlocks after mission 10.

    Look and feel

    • NewA guided tour shows where new features are and what to do next.
    • NewDisplay quality setting: Auto, High, Balanced or Battery saver. Auto picks the best sharpness for your phone.
    • ImprovedAfter the prologue you choose your name and go straight to mission 2.
    • ImprovedNotifications wait until a tip or the tour is finished.
    • ImprovedGame cards no longer show a NEW label. The Heist card shows your heists won.
    • ImprovedThe mission card has a big, full-width mission button.
    • ImprovedCoins and kills in the HUD have fixed icons.
    • ImprovedDefend together and Spectate are hidden until they are ready.

    Fixes

    • FixedDeleting your account now removes all your progress for good.
    • FixedExtra barrel bullets now meet at the target instead of spreading out and missing.
    • FixedAn invisible chest button covered the Play button.
    • FixedThe game cards no longer jump to the daily challenge by themselves.
    • FixedWrong texts on the first run of the day and the event title.
    • FixedThe name screen no longer cuts off its corners.
    • FixedShared score cards now use the name Mob Holdout.
    • FixedNotifications no longer cover tutorial tips.
    • FixedSome icons showed as emoji.
    • FixedThe district income shown now matches what you get.
