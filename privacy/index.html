Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data AstosStudio processes when you play Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense, visit this website or email us, why we do that, and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy covers Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense and this website. Daily Puzzle Club, Rocket Rain and Kermis Jackpot are still in development; we’ll add them here before they’re released.

Who we are

AstosStudio is a Dutch sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 93013116. AstosStudio is the controller for the processing described here.

Questions about privacy or your data: support@astosstudio.com.

Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

What we process

  • Anonymous player account. On first launch the game creates an account without a name, email address or phone number. You only get a random player ID.
  • Game progress. Your progress, your scores for the leaderboards and the player name you choose. Other players can see your player name and scores.
  • Family. If you start or join a family: the family name and tag, your rank, donations and points. Other players see your player name and rank.
  • Chat and private messages. For each message: your player ID and name (for a private message also those of the recipient), the family or conversation, your family rank, the text (at most 200 characters) or a quick message or emote, the language of your device and the time. Family chat is visible to the members of that family; a private message only to you and the other player.
  • Message settings. Who may send you private messages: everyone, your family or nobody.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a player, family or message: your player ID, who or what you reported, the name at that moment, the reason and the context (for a chat message also its text), plus the status, a staff note and the time. When you block a player: your player ID, the blocked player’s ID and the time. Your save, on your device and in your cloud save, also keeps the players and families you blocked (ID and name) and how far you have read your private conversations.
  • Suspensions. If we suspend an account for breaking the rules: the player ID, the reason and the time.
  • Gameplay statistics. Which modes you play, how far you get, which screens you open and technical errors. If you report or translate something, we only record that it happened, with the type and reason or the language pair, never the text. These statistics are linked to your random player ID, not to your name. You can turn this off in Settings › Share anonymous gameplay data.
  • Purchases. Payments go through Apple or Google. Through RevenueCat we only receive which purchase you made and whether a subscription such as VIP is active. We never receive your payment details.
  • Ads. Optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob. In the EU and EEA the game first asks for your consent. Without consent you get non-personalised ads; Google may still use limited data, such as your IP address and device information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On iPhone the game separately asks whether it may track you for ads. You can change that at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.

Keeping chat safe

Names and messages with offensive words are blocked, and messages containing phone numbers, email addresses or links are not sent. Our staff only read messages when they are reported, and can hide messages and suspend accounts that break the rules.

The Translate button

Under a message in another language you can tap Translate. Only then does your device send the text of that one message and the two languages (the sender’s language, or automatic detection, and the language of your device) directly to MyMemory, a translation service of Translated srl in Italy. MyMemory sees your IP address, but never your player ID or name. We don’t store the translation: it only stays in the game’s memory while the game is open. Translated’s own privacy policy applies to what MyMemory does with the request.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from this site itself. The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which processes your IP address in its server logs to deliver and protect the site.

We count page views ourselves, without cookies and without storing your IP address. For each page view we only store the page, the website you came from (for example google.com), your browser language, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the time. This shows us which pages are useful; it can’t be traced back to you. The counts are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, which processes your IP address briefly in its request logs to deliver and protect the service, and we delete them after 13 months. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don’t count your visit.

Legal basis for both: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

When you email us

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and any attachments to help you. Our email is hosted by TransIP in the Netherlands. We keep support emails until your question has been handled, and then for at most two years so we can recognise recurring problems. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract when your question is about a purchase.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase: game server, database and website statistics, located in the EU (Frankfurt). Processor.
  • RevenueCat: handling of purchases and subscriptions, United States. Processor.
  • Google (AdMob): ads. For personalised ads Google acts as an independent controller; see Google’s privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google: app stores and payments, each as an independent controller.
  • Translated srl (MyMemory): translation of a message when you tap Translate, Italy (EU). The request goes directly from your device to MyMemory.
  • Other players: see your player name, scores and rank, the messages in your family chat and the private messages you send them.
  • GitHub: hosting of this website, United States. Processor.
  • TransIP: email hosting, the Netherlands. Processor.

When data goes to the United States, this happens with appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep your data

We keep your game data for as long as your account exists, with these exceptions:

  • Chat and private messages: 30 days.
  • Reports: 12 months.
  • Suspensions: until they are lifted.
  • Blocks: until you undo them.

You can delete everything yourself in Settings › Delete account and data. This immediately removes from our server your account, progress, scores, family data, message settings, blocks, gameplay statistics, your messages (also in conversations with other players), and both the reports you made and the reports about you. If you lead a family, leadership passes to the member with the highest rank. Read more on deleting your account.

Purchases remain recorded with Apple or Google, because they are part of your store account. Cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play settings; deleting your game account does not cancel them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing (including the gameplay statistics), and to data portability. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that already took place.

Email support@astosstudio.com to use these rights. Your account has no name or email address, so please include your player ID (in Settings) or player name so we can find your data. We respond within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

Mob Holdout is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 16, only agree to personalised ads with permission from a parent or guardian.

Security

Connections between the game, our server and this website are encrypted, and only we have access to the management environments of our services.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We announce important changes in the game.
