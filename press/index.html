About the game

In one line: Defend your street, build your city, raid rivals and lead your mafia family.

The mob is coming for your street. You are the last line of defense.

Mob Holdout is a fast, neon-soaked defense game. Man the turret behind your wall, pick the right upgrades and stop wave after wave of gangsters, armored trucks and mafia bosses before they break through.

Your estate is your home base: a glowing 3D city that grows with you. Upgrade your mansion, vault, towers and more, and collect income while you are away. When you are ready to go on the attack, pick your squad, break through the gate of a rival estate and take the loot. Climb the divisions, earn trophies and defend your own estate against other players.

Along the way you recruit eight Capos, each with a unique super attack, perks and mastery levels. Found or join a family to chat, help each other, research bonuses together and take on the weekly Family boss.

There is no energy system. Everything can be earned by playing; optional purchases and rewarded ads help you progress faster and are never required.

Key features

  • Hold the line. One turret, one wall, one street. Dozens of upgrades to choose from mid-fight (chain lightning, ricochet bullets, drone support, explosive rounds, mortars and more) that combine into powerful evolutions.
  • Build your city. Your home screen is your own 3D city at night. Upgrade your mansion, vault, towers and more, and collect income while you are away.
  • Raid rival estates. Pick your squad, break through the gate and take the loot. Climb the divisions, earn trophies and defend your own estate against other players.
  • Recruit legendary Capos. Eight hand-drawn Capos, each with a unique super attack, perks and mastery levels.
  • 1,000 missions and Endless mode. 20 chapters, three stars to earn on every mission, and an Endless mode to see how long you can hold out.
  • Lead your family. Found or join a family: chat, help each other, research bonuses together and fight the weekly Family boss.
  • Seasons and events. A 50-tier season pass, a season boss, daily rewards and events.
  • Fair to play. No energy system. No paid loot boxes. Everything can be earned by playing.
  • 14 languages. English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
