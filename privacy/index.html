Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data AstosStudio processes when you play Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense, visit this website or email us, why we do that, and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy covers Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense and this website. Daily Puzzle Club, Rocket Rain and Kermis Jackpot are still in development; we’ll add them here before they’re released.

Who we are

AstosStudio is a Dutch sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 93013116. AstosStudio is the controller for the processing described here.

Questions about privacy or your data: support@astosstudio.com.

Mob Holdout: Mafia Defense

What we process

  • Anonymous player account. On first launch the game creates an account without a name, email address or phone number. You only get a random player ID.
  • Linking your account (optional). In Settings › Account you can link your account to an email address, or to your Apple or Google account, so you can sign in on another device. We then store that email address and, if you use Apple or Google, the identifier they give us (with Apple you can hide your email address; we then get a relay address). We use them only to sign you in and to send you the sign-in code. Legal basis: performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)). We delete them when you delete your account.
  • Game progress. Your progress, your scores for the leaderboards and the player name you choose. Other players can see your player name and scores.
  • Public profile. Other players can open your profile. It shows your player name and family tag, the avatar, frame, banner and title you chose, your turret skin, whether you have VIP, your reputation, your favourite Capo and featured achievements, the stars and mastery of your Capos, and a few totals such as your highest mission, stars, kills, games played, power and trophies. We don’t show the profile of a suspended or hidden account.
  • Family. If you start or join a family: the family name and tag, your rank, donations and points. Other players see your player name and rank. For the weekly Family boss we store your player name and the damage you did that week.
  • City raid. Your estate’s defence (building levels, trap lane and skin), your crew and Capo, your trophies and division, and your shield. For each raid: who attacked whom, the time, the attacker’s moves, the result, and the loot and trophies. A player who finds your estate sees your player name, family, estate rank, defence, skin and trophies. Attacker and defender see the raid in their log and can replay it.
  • Inviting friends (optional). Your invite code. If a new player enters it, we store that your two accounts are linked, how far your friend has got in the missions and which rewards were collected. You see your friends’ player names and progress; your friend sees your player name.
  • Playing together and spectating. In Defend together, and when members of your family watch your game live, your player name and the state of your game (such as mode, wave, score and what happens on the battlefield) are passed on live through our server to the players in that room. We don’t store this. You can turn spectating off in Settings › Allow live spectating.
  • Chat and private messages. For each message: your player ID and name (for a private message also those of the recipient), the family or conversation, your family rank, the text (at most 200 characters) or a quick message or emote, the language of your device and the time. Family chat is visible to the members of that family; a private message only to you and the other player.
  • Message settings. Who may send you private messages: everyone, your family or nobody.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a player, family or message: your player ID, who or what you reported, the name at that moment, the reason and the context (for a chat message also its text), plus the status, a staff note and the time. When you block a player: your player ID, the blocked player’s ID and the time. Your save, on your device and in your cloud save, also keeps the players and families you blocked (ID and name) and how far you have read your private conversations.
  • Suspensions. If we suspend an account for breaking the rules: the player ID, the reason and the time.
  • Fair play. For each game you play: the mode, the mission level, the start and end time, your score, wave and kills, and whether the score was within our limits. Also daily totals of your family donations, faction points, family war attacks and quest contributions. With this we check that leaderboard scores and family points are possible. The game uses the time of our server, not the clock of your phone.
  • Check-ins and checking your save. While you play, the game regularly checks in with our server: at least once a minute, after each wave, and around raids and purchases. We store a random device mark that the game creates itself (so an account is played on one device at a time), the game version, when you were last active and how long you played that day, and for each game your progress per wave (time, wave, kills, score, wall and coins). Each time your progress is saved, our server compares it with the previous save and with what it knows itself: your games, missions, rewards, diamond spending and purchases. What can’t be right is logged with the figures involved and can be set back. If we establish cheating, we can suspend an account or hide it from other players (on leaderboards and as a raid target).
  • Diamonds and rewards. Your diamond balance is kept on our server, with a log of each change: what it was for, how many, and the balance afterwards. We also record which rewards you collected (per type and day, or once per reward), so that each reward counts only once.
  • Gameplay statistics. Which modes you play, how far you get, which screens you open and technical errors. If you report or translate something, we only record that it happened, with the type and reason or the language pair, never the text. Once per session we also record your device model, screen size and frame rate, so we can tune the graphics per device. These statistics are linked to your random player ID, not to your name. You can turn this off in Settings › Share anonymous gameplay data.
  • Purchases. Payments go through Apple or Google. RevenueCat reports each purchase, renewal and refund to our server: the product, the store’s transaction number, the price and currency, the time and, for a subscription such as VIP, its end date. With this we add what you bought to your account and can restore it. We never receive your payment details.
  • Ads. Optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob. In the EU and EEA the game first asks for your consent. Without consent you get non-personalised ads; Google may still use limited data, such as your IP address and device information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On iPhone the game separately asks whether it may track you for ads. You can change that at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone. When you watch a rewarded ad, the game gives Google your random player ID, so Google can confirm to our server that the ad was watched. For each confirmed ad we store Google’s transaction number, the ad unit, the reward and the time.
  • Notifications (optional). Most notifications are planned by your phone itself; nothing is sent to us for those. To tell you that another player raided your estate, we need your device’s push address: if you allow notifications, the game registers it with us, together with the platform (iOS or Android), your language and your time zone. We log when such a notification was sent. You can turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications.

Keeping chat safe

Names and messages with offensive words are blocked, and messages containing phone numbers, email addresses or links are not sent. Our staff only read messages when they are reported, and can hide messages and suspend accounts that break the rules.

The Translate button

Under a message in another language you can tap Translate. Only then does your device send the text of that one message and the two languages (the sender’s language, or automatic detection, and the language of your device) directly to MyMemory, a translation service of Translated srl in Italy. MyMemory sees your IP address, but never your player ID or name. We don’t store the translation: it only stays in the game’s memory while the game is open. Translated’s own privacy policy applies to what MyMemory does with the request.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from this site itself. The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which processes your IP address in its server logs to deliver and protect the site.

We count page views ourselves, without cookies and without storing your IP address. For each page view we only store the page, the website you came from (for example google.com), your browser language, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the time. This shows us which pages are useful; it can’t be traced back to you. The counts are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, which processes your IP address briefly in its request logs to deliver and protect the service, and we delete them after 13 months. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don’t count your visit.

Legal basis for both: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

When you email us

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and any attachments to help you. Our email is hosted by TransIP in the Netherlands. We keep support emails until your question has been handled, and then for at most two years so we can recognise recurring problems. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract when your question is about a purchase.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase: game server, database and website statistics, located in the EU (Frankfurt). Processor.
  • RevenueCat: handling of purchases and subscriptions, United States. Processor.
  • Google (AdMob): ads. For personalised ads Google acts as an independent controller; see Google’s privacy policy. With a rewarded ad Google also receives your random player ID, to confirm the ad to our server.
  • Apple and Google: app stores and payments, each as an independent controller.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivery of notifications about raids, United States. Expo receives your device’s push address and the text of the notification (with the attacker’s player name) and passes them on to Apple or Google, who deliver the notification to your phone. Processor.
  • Translated srl (MyMemory): translation of a message when you tap Translate, Italy (EU). The request goes directly from your device to MyMemory.
  • Other players: see your player name, scores and rank, your public profile, your estate when they raid you and the raids between you, your progress if one of you invited the other, your game when you play together or your family watches live, the messages in your family chat and the private messages you send them.
  • GitHub: hosting of this website, United States. Processor.
  • TransIP: email hosting, the Netherlands. Processor.

When data goes to the United States, this happens with appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep your data

We keep your game data for as long as your account exists, with these exceptions:

  • Chat and private messages: 30 days.
  • Reports: 12 months.
  • Suspensions: until they are lifted.
  • Blocks: until you undo them.
  • Game results for fair play: 35 days.
  • Daily family totals: 14 days.
  • City raids: 35 days.
  • Damage to the Family boss: 8 weeks.
  • Diamond log: 90 days.
  • Daily reward counters: 14 days. Confirmed ads: 30 days.
  • Fair-play log (what was logged or set back): 90 days.
  • Push address: until you turn notifications off, or 120 days after it was last renewed. Log of sent notifications: 30 days.

You can delete everything yourself in Settings › Delete account and data. This immediately removes from our server your account, progress, scores, family data, message settings, blocks, gameplay statistics, game results, your messages (also in conversations with other players), and both the reports you made and the reports about you. It also removes your public profile, your estate and raids, your invite code and invites, your diamond balance and log, the purchases we recorded, the reward and fair-play logs, and your device’s push address. If you lead a family, leadership passes to the member with the highest rank. Read more on deleting your account.

Purchases remain recorded with Apple or Google, because they are part of your store account. Cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play settings; deleting your game account does not cancel them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing (including the gameplay statistics), and to data portability. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that already took place.

Email support@astosstudio.com to use these rights. Unless you linked your account, it has no name or email address, so please include your player ID (in Settings) or player name so we can find your data. We respond within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

Mob Holdout is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 16, only agree to personalised ads with permission from a parent or guardian.

Security

Connections between the game, our server and this website are encrypted, and only we have access to the management environments of our services.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing. Our fair-play checks decide automatically whether a score, a reward or a change to your progress or diamonds counts, and can set your saved progress back to what is possible; a person always decides on suspending an account or hiding it from other players.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We announce important changes in the game.
